A queued request claimed by a controller is now a lease: an entry whose claim was never confirmed — the claim's reply was lost, or its controller died before dispatching it — is handed out again after `QUEUE_CLAIM_LEASE_MS` rather than sitting `inProgress` until its admission times out, and an entry the backend cannot decode is passed over rather than claimed. A freeze that still owes thaw probes now outlives its window by up to a week, re-armed by every probe that succeeds and by the fresh freeze a failed probe arms, rather than expiring a minute after the window and letting the client back to full rate unprobed. The completion ids that keep a redelivered probe from counting twice are kept as long.

Settlement forgives refill discarded at the bucket's ceiling once in total rather than once per outstanding claim, so overlapping settlements are charged in full, and a newer claim settling first no longer costs an older one the refill discarded before the newer was made; it no longer applies to a bucket that expired and was recreated since the claim, or to a mark that carries no epoch; and the Redis backend reports `applied` and `unapplied` exactly. Fractional costs are admitted within a tolerance of `BALANCE_TOLERANCE` times the smaller of the cost and `maxTokens`, so a bucket of 1 admits a hundred claims of 0.01 while a spent bucket with a large ceiling admits nothing more. A claim mark whose lifetime cannot be stored is refused before anything is spent on both backends.

The Redis backend honours an ioredis `keyPrefix` on its connection everywhere: instance discovery, frozen-grant cleanup and every pub/sub channel, so two fleets that differ only by that prefix no longer read each other's peers or messages, and a claim with no mark or a refund with no freeze key no longer reads or writes the key the bare prefix names. `subscribe([])` is a no-op on both backends. The memory backend now matches Redis on a stored `"inf"` priority, on per-limit queue stats for limits named `constructor` or `__proto__`, on the byte order of same-score entries, and on the lifetime of an emptied bucket whose config cannot refill.

BREAKING: a key TTL of zero or below, or a non-finite one, passed to any rate-limit operation — the fast paths, `acquireTokens`, `acquireMultiLimit`, `touchConcurrency`, `touchClaimMark`, `addRequest` and claim marks — throws `RangeError` before anything is written, on both backends; it used to expire the shared key, other holders' slots with it. The plain store keeps reading zero or below as "delete now". For a custom `DianemoBackend`: the new required `confirmRequest` ends a claim's lease and must refuse one that has lapsed; `getNextRequest` must stamp `QueuedRequest.claimedAt` and hand out again an entry whose stamp is older than `QUEUE_CLAIM_LEASE_MS`; a status written through `updateRequest` must clear the stamp; a bucket must keep the `epoch` and the running total `forgiven` that `settleTokens` reads, each copied into a claim mark when it is written; `hasThawRequestInProgress` and `tryStartThawRequest` must not count a probe whose queue claim has lapsed; and `updateThawProgress` must leave a freeze untouched, its lifetime included, when the probe failed. Run the suite in `test/conformance.test.ts` against it.
